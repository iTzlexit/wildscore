# How to play

> This is the source for the in-app **How to play** screen. Written as player-
> facing copy, not developer notes — what is here should be readable straight
> off the phone. Add to it as rules are settled.

---

## The short version

Everyone in the car plays. You spot animals, you claim them, the rarest finds
win. At the end of the day the scores are added up and the argument is over.

---

## Starting a day

**Start a scorecard** and add everyone in the vehicle by name. No accounts, no
sign-ups — just names.

One scorecard runs at a time, and it belongs to the car, not to a person.

## Claiming a sighting

Whoever calls it first gets it.

Tap the animal, tap the name of whoever shouted. It is timestamped, so "I said
it first" has an answer. You have five minutes to reassign a mis-tap, then the
claim locks.

## Not everything can be claimed forever

Impala are everywhere. If every impala counted, the game would be over by the
first waterhole.

So **common animals run out.** By default, an animal in the lower tiers can be
claimed a limited number of times per day. Once its chances are used up the
tile locks for the rest of the day, and everyone moves on to looking for
something better.

**Rare animals never run out.** Every leopard counts, every single time.

## Points

Each species is worth points based on how genuinely hard it is to find in
Kruger — not how famous it is. Lions are easier to find than sable.

| Tier | Points |
|---|---|
| Common | 5 |
| Frequent | 15 |
| Notable | 30 |
| Rare | 60 |
| Very rare | 100 |
| Exceptional | 200 |
| Legendary | 400 |

**First call of the day is worth double.** Whoever gets the first animal of the
morning, whatever it is, takes twice the points. Being awake at the gate should
count for something.

## Bonuses

- **The Big Five in one day** — first player to claim lion, leopard, elephant,
  buffalo and rhino on the same day takes **+400**.
- **Big Six Birds in one day** — martial eagle, lappet-faced vulture,
  saddle-billed stork, kori bustard, ground hornbill and Pel's fishing owl.
  **+400**, and considerably harder than the Big Five.

## Winning

Points carry across every day of your trip. The winner is whoever has the most
when you drive out of the gate.

## Beyond your car

Your daily score is submitted when you next have signal and appears on the
**Leaderboard**, ranked against other players across the park.

Most of Kruger has no coverage, so this happens back at camp rather than in the
moment — the game itself works entirely offline.

---

## Two ways to play

### Park rules

The standard scoring above, unchanged. **Only Park rules games appear on the
public leaderboard**, because a shared ranking needs everyone playing the same
game.

### House rules

Your car, your game. Before you start you can:

- **Leave species out entirely** — if nobody wants to count guinea fowl, drop it
- **Change how many chances** an animal gets before it locks
- **Change what things are worth** — if you think cheetah deserves more than
  leopard, say so

House games are scored, saved and ranked **within your vehicle**. They do not
go on the public leaderboard, for the obvious reason: a score is only
comparable to another score if both were earned under the same rules.

Nothing is lost by playing house rules. Sightings still enter your personal
record.
