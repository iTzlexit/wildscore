How hard is it to spot?

Help us rank Kruger's animals

Wild Score is a scorecard game for Kruger — you spot animals on a drive and score points based on how hard each one is to find. We are trying to get that ranking right, and the people who actually know are the people who drive the park.

You do not have to do all of them. Change only the ones you think are wrong. Even five corrections help.

Rank by how hard the animal is to find, not how impressive it is. A leopard is exciting; the question is how many days you would drive before seeing one.

Thank you.
